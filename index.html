Our Story

A shop built the way a neighbourhood needs one

Saraswathi Fancy Store & Gift Center started the way most good neighborhood fancy stores do — as a warm, dedicated counter that carried a bit of everything, run by people who knew that a household rarely needs just one thing at a time. A bangle set here, a packet of diyas there, a birthday card or hair ribbon picked up on the way home.

Over the years the counter grew into a full shopfront on Chunchagatta Main Road, Mandya Complex, but the philosophy never changed: Stock what a family actually reaches for, keep prices honest, and know the regulars by name.

Today the shop is arranged into 8 clear aisles — cosmetics separated from pooja items, toys away from stationery — so a quick morning visit stays fast, and a relaxed evening browse still turns up a delightful surprise.
